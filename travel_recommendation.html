Our Story

About Wanderlust

Who We Are

Born From a Passion for Discovery

Wanderlust was founded in 2018 by a group of seasoned travellers who believed that discovering the world's most extraordinary places shouldn't require endless hours of research. We set out to build a platform that connects curious explorers with authentic, curated travel recommendations.

From hidden beaches in Southeast Asia to centuries-old temples and the cultural heartbeats of continents — our recommendations are personally vetted, beautifully presented, and always honest.

Today, Wanderlust serves millions of travellers across the globe, helping them craft journeys that are not just trips, but transformative life experiences.
